Realistic Lunar Colony Political Sci Fi Series

Citizens of Luna

Citizens of Luna is a political science fiction series about the first permanent human city on the Moon and the long, dirty fight over who controls it. Addison Port writes the story of a colony caught between Earthbound powers, corporate interests, and its own people who are tired of being treated like a distant asset.

Luna as a Living System

The books treat the lunar city as a living system: every airlock, trade agreement, and media feed has consequences on the ground.

Diplomats, saboteurs, engineers, and everyday citizens collide in a slow-burn conflict that moves from quiet pressure to outright struggle for independence.

Addison’s writing relies on rigorous worldbuilding, detailing the scientific constraints and logistical realities of living in a low-gravity vacuum, without letting the science bog down the pacing.

About the Pen Name

Meet Addison Port

Addison Port writes grounded, politically charged science fiction about off-world colonies, contested sovereignty, and the people trying to build a life in the middle of it all.

If you like your space fiction more about power, infrastructure, and ideals than alien invasions, Citizens of Luna is where to start.
