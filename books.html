Addison Port presents

Citizens of Luna

Grounded Political Science Fiction Series

Citizens of Luna follows a realistic lunar colony from fragile founding through uneasy prosperity to a full-scale fight over who gets to rule.

It’s political, tense, and grounded in the idea that every piece of infrastructure—from airlocks to data links—has a human cost.

Glory’s Whetstone

Brutal Sword & Sandal / Gay Romance Series
