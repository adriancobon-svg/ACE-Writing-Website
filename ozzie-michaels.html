Gritty Detroit Organized Crime Thriller Series

The Pained Syndicate

Detroit is a city where history never really lets go, and The Pained Syndicate is the crew still paying the bill. Ozzie Michaels writes gritty organized crime thrillers about the bosses, fixers, and loyal screwups trying to survive under the weight of those old debts in a city that sees too much.

The Surveillance Battlefield

The books treat modern surveillance and technology as part of the battlefield: every smartphone, license plate reader, and traffic cam is another angle where things can go wrong.

When the shadows get smaller, criminals have to get smarter or more desperate, and The Pained Syndicate pushes both to the breaking point.

Ozzie’s thrillers take place in the present-day Midwest, capturing the architectural beauty, dark alleys, and cold winter winds of Detroit.

About the Pen Name

Meet Ozzie Michaels

Ozzie Michaels writes hard-edged crime fiction set in present-day Detroit, with an eye for tight crews, sharp dialogue, and the quiet moments where loyalty cracks.

If you grew up on gritty thrillers and want that same energy in a world wired for constant watching, this is your universe.
