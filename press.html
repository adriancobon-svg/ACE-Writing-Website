Press & Media Kit

Assets, bios, and interview angles for the ACE Writing imprint

This page exists to make life easier for book bloggers, reviewers, podcasters, and journalists who want to cover ACE Writing, Adrian Cobon, or any of the three pen names.

You’ll find ready-to-use author bios, series descriptions, and download links for photos and covers once they’re available.

Identity & Imprint Ownership

ACE Writing is the imprint of Adrian Cobon, who also writes as Ozzie Michaels (Detroit noir crime), Addison Port (lunar colony science fiction), and Titus Marcellus (sword & sandal fantasy with a gay protagonist). For legal and media purposes, all titles and pen names listed here are written and controlled by Adrian Cobon.

Author Bios

Please feel free to use these bios in any reviews, interviews, or features. They can be edited for length if necessary.

Short Bio (copy-paste friendly)

"Adrian Cobon is a professional editor and copywriter based in Indianapolis, Indiana, and the writer behind the ACE Writing imprint. Publishing as Ozzie Michaels, Addison Port, and Titus Marcellus, he writes gritty Detroit organized crime thrillers, realistic lunar colony political science fiction, and brutal sword & sandal fantasy centered on queer characters."

Longer Bio (copy-paste friendly)

"Adrian Cobon spent more than sixteen years in professional editing and copywriting before launching ACE Writing, a one-person imprint for pulpy, character-driven genre fiction. Under the pen names Ozzie Michaels, Addison Port, and Titus Marcellus, he writes about Detroit crime syndicates in an age of constant surveillance, lunar colonies wrestling with independence, and gladiators who would rather burn an empire than hide who they are."

Suggested Interview Topics

If you're hosting Adrian on your podcast or blog, here are a few suggested discussion angles:

  • Editorial Discipline & Pulp Speed: How sixteen years in professional editing and copywriting shaped Adrian’s approach to pulp fiction, worldbuilding, and disciplined drafting.
  • Surveillance in Crime Fiction: What modern surveillance and technology do to crime fiction and the shrinking shadows criminals rely on in The Pained Syndicate.
  • Lunar Colony Geopolitics: How colonial history and independence movements informed the politics of Citizens of Luna and the off-world struggle for self-governance.
