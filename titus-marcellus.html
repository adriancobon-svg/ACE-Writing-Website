Glory’s Whetstone

Glory’s Whetstone is a sword & sandal epic about a captured warrior forced into the arena and the men he refuses to stop loving, even when the empire wants them dead. Titus Marcellus writes brutal, sensual fantasy in a world of sand, bronze, and blood where the crowd is hungry and the gods don’t answer.

Spectacle and Rebellion

The books blend the spectacle of gladiator combat with the slow churn of a Spartacus-style slave revolt, told from the perspective of a proudly gay protagonist.

Desire, loyalty, and rage all share the same blade, and every victory in the arena comes with a cost outside its walls.

Titus Marcellus builds a rich historical-fantasy setting that honors classical Mediterranean cultures while weaving in low-magic fantasy elements and uncompromising emotional stakes.

About the Pen Name

Meet Titus Marcellus

Titus Marcellus writes sword & sandal fantasy that embraces queer protagonists and refuses to apologize for heat, tenderness, or brutality.

If you grew up on Conan and gladiator epics and always wished the hero could love another man, this universe was made for you.
